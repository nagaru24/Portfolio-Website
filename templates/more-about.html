More About Me

My name, Nagaru Okawa (大川悠流), has a unique meaning.

In Japanese, “Okawa” (大川) means “big river”, and “Nagaru” (悠流) means “flowing eternally”. My father, a Japanese teacher, gave me this name to inspire me to keep moving forward and stay pure like flowing water.

I was born and raised in Hokkaido, Japan, the northernmost prefecture. I played baseball for 16 years, which led me to study at Juntendo University in the biomechanics program.

In a biomechanics lab, I focused on analyzing baseball performance and became fascinated by data analysis, motion tracking, and coding that help athletes improve and prevent injury.

To connect technology, data, and business, I am pursuing a degree in Management Information Systems (MIS) at the University of Nebraska at Omaha.

Moving to the U.S. has expanded my knowledge and prepared me for a future career in IT. The journey from athlete to technology enthusiast has shaped my passion for continuous learning and using technology to solve real-world problems.
